The expert pack is the on-disk format. Think of it as a carefully organized archive: a header, an index, and then one blob per expert, each sitting at a known position and aligned to disk-friendly four-kilobyte boundaries — so any expert can be grabbed with one clean read, no unpacking. Weights are quantized to four bits in groups of sixty-four, laid out so the GPU can stream them linearly. The model's shared core ships as a separate file, which — as you'll see later — is what makes switching models take under a second.
