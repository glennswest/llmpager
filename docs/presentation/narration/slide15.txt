And then the part that goes beyond the original inspiration: multi-model serving. Two thirty-billion-parameter models — a general one and a coding specialist, thirty-seven gigabytes of weights combined — both warm on the sixteen-gigabyte card, behind a standard OpenAI-compatible API. A budgeter divides the cache: a model running alone gets the big cache; when a second one warms up, both shrink to share. And loading a model into rotation takes point-eight seconds, because only its three-gigabyte core actually moves — the experts page themselves in as they're used.
