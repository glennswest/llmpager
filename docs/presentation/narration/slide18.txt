Lesson three is about process. Correctness first: every kernel verified before any tuning, which made optimization safe. Prove the ceiling first: we measured what paging could deliver before writing a line of model code, so we always knew where the wall wasn't. And treat negative results as deliverables — the rejected experiments are documented with numbers right next to the wins. The whole ladder — paging proof, real tokens, doubling performance, serving, multi-model — went from an empty repository to ten tagged releases in one evening.
