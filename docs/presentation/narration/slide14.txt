This is my favorite result. Perplexity measures how surprised the model is by real text, lower is better, and it's exquisitely sensitive to any corruption of the weights. We measured it at two different cache sizes: identical, to four decimal places. That's the proof that paging is lossless, the cache changes when weights arrive, never what they are. It also priced an experiment we'd rejected: quantizing the core cost four and a half percent in quality on top of being slower. Now we knew both costs, with numbers.
