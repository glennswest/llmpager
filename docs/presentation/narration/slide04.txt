Before going further, six terms in plain language. A token is a word-piece; tokens per second is basically writing speed. Weights are the billions of learned numbers that are the model. VRAM is the GPU's own fast memory — small but very fast. Mixture-of-experts means each layer has a hundred and twenty-eight small specialist networks, and a router picks the best eight for each token. Quantization stores weights in four bits instead of sixteen — like compressing a photo. And paging is the desk-and-filing-cabinet trick every operating system uses: keep what you're using on the desk, fetch the rest when asked.
