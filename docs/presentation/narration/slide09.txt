The pager is the heart of the system — think of it as a team of librarians. Each worker owns its own file handle, staging buffer, and GPU stream. When the model asks for eight experts, whatever's already on the desk is handed over instantly, and the rest arrive from the cabinet in under two milliseconds — while the GPU keeps working on something else. Measured on its own, the pager can feed experts at a hundred and thirteen tokens per second — three times faster than the compute currently consumes them. Nobody ever stands around waiting.
