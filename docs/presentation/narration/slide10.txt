Kernels are the small programs that run on the GPU and do the actual arithmetic, matrix multiplies, normalization, attention, and so on. There are twelve of them, and every single one was verified against a slow but trusted CPU implementation before any optimization happened. Worst disagreement across the whole set: about two parts per million. That discipline paid off later, once correctness was established, every performance change could be judged purely on speed, because the output provably didn't change.
