Lesson one: measure, don't guess. Three confident, plausible ideas; three rejections by data. Quantizing the core to four bits should have saved four-x the bandwidth — it was slower, because the unpack cost ate the savings. Prefetching the next layer's experts should have hidden latency — it nearly halved throughput, because wrong prefetches evict good cache entries and steal disk bandwidth. And more I/O threads made the disk slower, not faster. Best of all: the same core-quantization idea wins in one workload and loses in another. Measure the workload you ship.
