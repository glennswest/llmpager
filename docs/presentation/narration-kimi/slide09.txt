War story two is my favorite bug of the project. First full run. Every layer executes. Seven hundred twenty seven gigabytes stream through the card. And the model says foss foss foss foss, forever. The cause? The compressed tensors format stores four bit integers as offset binary, value plus eight. I had assumed two's complement. One sign convention, and all five hundred seventy gigabytes of experts decoded as noise. The worst part? My unit test passed, because it validated my encoder against my own decoder, both wrong together. A differential test against the official library caught it in minutes. And the fix made the repack simpler, a verbatim copy.
